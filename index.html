CASE 01
React NativeFirebaseTypeScript9 countries
Client · Self-service home

GPS Field Operations Platform

A cross-platform operations app for a multi-country logistics and support-services company — architected and shipped single-handed.

● Actual screens from the production app I built
Ⅰ Problem

Vehicle and accommodation handovers, exit permits and procurement ran on paper and email across nine countries, while a legacy osTicket helpdesk sat siloed on its own PHP/MySQL stack — no unified system, no audit trail.

Ⅱ Approach

Built a React Native / Expo app over 32 Node 22 Cloud Functions and a 16-collection Firestore model, giving clients one country-routed queue to raise and track fleet, accommodation and IT maintenance requests. Added TOTP MFA from primitives, bridged the legacy osTicket helpdesk through a VPC connector with a static egress IP, and used the Claude Vision API to flag vehicle damage from handover photos.

Ⅲ Result

~24,000 lines of TypeScript across 59 screens, deployed across 9 countries — with five permission tiers and signed-URL, VirusTotal-scanned document delivery, consistent with DFARS and NIST SP 800-171.

CASE 02
CMMC L2NIST 800-171DFARSSSP · POA&M

CMMC Level 2 Compliance Program

The information-security program that keeps the company eligible to work with DoD prime contractors.

Ⅰ Problem

Contracts with DoD primes carry DFARS 252.204-7012 obligations. Without a documented, self-assessed CMMC posture across the NIST SP 800-171 control families, the company's eligibility for that work was at risk.

Ⅱ Approach

Authored the full IT policy and procedure set for CMMC Level 1 and 2, produced the System Security Plan, staff security handbook, compliance forms and a CUI scoping analysis, and ran the organization's Level 2 self-assessment end to end.

Ⅲ Result

A 110/110 SPRS score (UID S200028618) — a perfect Level 2 self-assessment — plus a POA&M mapping the remediation path to Smartsheet Gov and Microsoft 365 GCC High.

CASE 03
Telecom700+ linesVendor MgmtMulti-country

Multi-Carrier Telecom Consolidation

Sourcing and rationalizing the connectivity that DoD contracts depend on in the field.

Ⅰ Problem

Internet and cellular service for contracts including CACI, Northrop Grumman and BME was spread across multiple carriers — Zain, STC and Ooredoo — over many sites and countries, with fragmented plans and no central control of spend.

Ⅱ Approach

Took ownership of sourcing and vendor management across all three carriers, standardized the line inventory site by site, and negotiated renewals to consolidate overlapping plans against measured usage.

Ⅲ Result

700+ voice and data lines brought under unified management across Zain, STC and Ooredoo — one point of accountability for field connectivity in every country of operation, with renewals consolidated against measured usage.
